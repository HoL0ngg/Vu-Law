## Our approach (`#our-approach`)

A headline, two paragraphs, then four steps (step name + description). Numbering is visual only; the client did not number them.

```text
Our approach
From legal issues to a course of action.
Every matter presents different circumstances, risks and objectives. We therefore do not apply a standard solution to every client.
Our approach follows four steps:
Understand the matter
Identify the facts, context, interests and practical objectives of the client.
Assess the position
Analyse the applicable law, evidence, risks and available options.
Develop the strategy
Determine the appropriate course of action by considering objectives, enforceability, cost, timing and potential consequences.
Execute with precision
Implement the strategy systematically, adapt when circumstances change and maintain clear communication throughout the engagement.
```
